SubSpace

( ’25 )

Subspace is an experimental platform where music, architecture, and community converge. It is not tied to a single venue but designed as a roaming frequency that redefines how sound is experienced. Each activation transforms its environment into an instrument, creating temporary spaces where people, space, and resonance connect. More than an event, Subspace is a living system, a shared circuit for those seeking deeper, immersive encounters with sound.

Scope

Subspace 37m² began with a simple idea: turning the limitations of a physical space into the foundation of an event concept. I contributed to shaping its identity from the ground up, developing the name and its underlying narrative around the 37-square-metre venue that hosted the first edition. Beyond naming, I was involved in curating the experience, coordinating its execution, and contributing to the visual identity at CURB Studio, helping translate the initial concept into a cohesive event experience.
