[A bit of background]

My path has moved through technology, design, brand building and community-led projects. I've worked around immersive media and digital experiences, then found myself increasingly interested in the bigger picture: the idea, the people, the story and how all the moving parts come together.

That mix still shapes how I approach things. I like working with people from different disciplines, asking better questions, and staying close enough to the making to see an idea through.
